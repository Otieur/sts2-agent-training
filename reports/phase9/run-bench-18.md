# Comparison: fights inside rollouts ended by the fight-outcome model, against fights played by the rule planner, at Ascension 0 (run benchmark 18)

Written before the run (decision 0005 item 4, ML-10), 2026-11-21 at 22:46, after the results of run benchmarks 2–9, 11 and 12 and before the results of run benchmarks 15, 16 and 17 (of run benchmark 15, the floors of its first two seeds and one seed's factor effects had been seen in the queue's log). Sections 1–4 are not edited after the run starts; corrections go in section 6 with the reason.

## 1. Hypotheses
- **H1 (floors, act horizon):** rollouts whose fights are ended by the fight-outcome model (`rfm`) reach more floors than rollouts whose fights are played by the rule planner.
- **H2 (floors, run horizon):** the same rollouts played on to the end of the run (`rfm+hz`) reach more floors than the planner's rollouts.
- **H3, H4 (wins):** each of the two wins more runs.

Why: the review of 2026-11-21 found that every run decision is judged by a weaker fighter than the one that plays. Inside rollouts the rule planner fights; it dies at bosses that search beats, so rollouts that reach a boss mostly end with the same value, options tie (13% of card decisions in run benchmark 11), and routes are chosen to keep away from fights the planner loses. The fight-outcome model answers for search instead: trained on fights search played, it gives the probability that search wins a fight from the deck, relics, potions, HP and encounter, and the HP left. An interim model on the 5,436 search fights recorded by this evening is ahead of per-encounter base rates on held-out runs (test: elites Brier 0.193 against 0.203 and AUC 0.80 against 0.74; bosses 0.244 against 0.255 and 0.67 against 0.51; validation: bosses 0.157 against 0.212), where the first model on 5,089 fights was not. In a smoke test its rollouts valued the first map move at about 21 (16 floors and most of a boss bonus), the planner's rollouts at 10 to 12; the stack itself reaches floor 31 on average and beats the act 1 boss in 25 of 30 runs. A rollout without fights is also four to six times cheaper, which pays for playing to the end of the run.

## 2. Runs
- 30 new seeds (`RB018` + index 00000–00029), Ironclad, **Ascension 0**, whole new runs in the simulator (`tools/run_bench.py`). Three policies play every seed.
- `F`: the stack after run benchmark 15 by that registration's rule (`data/learn/queue/stack2.py final`), fixed by the queue script before any result of this run. Arms: `F`, `F+rfm`, `F+rfm+hz`.
- `rfm` (`tools/run_eval.py`): in every rollout of the arm (card rewards, map moves, and shops and events where rollouts judge them) fights are not played. At a fight's first decision the model gives the probability of winning and the HP fraction left after a win; the fight is ended as won (host command `resolve_fight`, rollout worlds only, FI-1d) with that HP, at most 6 above the HP it started with, and with 0, 1 or 2 held potions spent in a hallway, elite or boss fight; the probability multiplies the rollout's chance of being alive. The rollout's value is the expectation of the planner rollouts' value: each floor climbed counts with the chance of being alive when it is reached, and beating the act's boss adds 5 + 5 × HP fraction with the chance of having survived it. No fight outcome is drawn. Everything outside fights is played by the stack's rules as before, on the same 6 sampled futures per option, to the end of the act.
- `hz`: the rollouts play on to the end of the run; every act boss beaten adds its bonus with the chance of having survived it.
- The model: `fight-v1c`, trained by the queue script right before this run with `tools/learn/train_fight.py` at its defaults (seed 0) on every fight search has played in the simulator up to then (`tools/learn/fight_data.py --teachers mcw6`: self-play sets, fight benchmarks, run benchmarks up to 15), split 70/15/15 by run. No model or setting is chosen by results: one training, used as it comes. Its held-out report goes into section 6 before any result of this run is read. This run's seeds are new, so none of its fights is in the model's data. The rollouts use the model's plain forward pass (`tools/learn/fight_predict.py`; checked against the trained model to 1e-5).
- The run's own fights: `mcw6` in every fight, `--fight-rebuild save+run`, in all arms. Host for all arms: the Release3 build, which is Release2's source plus `resolve_fight` (refused outside rollout worlds), after the same checks as Release2 (section 6). 7 workers claim seeds in index order (`--dynamic`). Stopping rule: unfinished runs stop 5 hours after the start; time the machine is asleep or off does not count. The analysis uses the seeds for which all three policies finished.
- Fights and rollout decisions are recorded as in run benchmark 11; each decision also records the rollout settings its evaluator used.

## 3. Policies
- `F`: fights inside rollouts played by the rule planner; value = floors climbed, plus 5 + 5 × HP fraction if the act's boss is beaten.
- `F+rfm`: fights inside rollouts ended by the model; the expected value, to the end of the act.
- `F+rfm+hz`: the same to the end of the run.

## 4. Metrics and tests
- H1 and H2: floor reached, paired by seed against `F`, one-sided sign test at 0.05 each, mean paired difference with a bootstrap 95% interval. H3 and H4: wins, one-sided exact sign test at 0.05 on the seeds where exactly one arm wins; wins per arm with Wilson intervals (`tools/run_details.py --wins-vs`). The two horizons are two questions; no correction across them. `F+rfm+hz` against `F+rfm` is reported, not tested.
- Checks that the treatment was applied: the rollout settings recorded with every decision (`fight_model`, `horizon`, `rollouts_by`) are each arm's own in every decision (`tools/run_details.py --decisions`); rollouts that failed per arm (`rollout_errors`).
- A check of the model on new fights: for every fight this run really plays, the model's win probability at the fight's start against the outcome under search, as Brier score and AUC beside the per-encounter base rates of the model's training data, by room type, with a calibration table.
- Secondary: how often all options of a decision have the same value, and how often the choice differs from the rule's, by kind of decision; elites fought per run and node types chosen; relics, upgrades, potions and HP at each boss fight's start; act bosses beaten; where runs die; seconds per run.
- What follows, the stack that runs next: of the two model arms, the one with the larger mean paired difference in floors against `F` enters the stack if that difference is positive and it does not win fewer runs than `F`; otherwise `F` stays. Run benchmark 16 then runs on that stack, and run benchmark 17 only if `rfm` is not in it (the boss credit has no meaning in rollouts that do not play the boss fight). A claim that the model's rollouts help needs its test.
