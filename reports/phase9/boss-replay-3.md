# Comparison: the end of a turn checked against the rule planner in the fight search (boss replay 3)

Written before the run (ML-10, decision 0006 item 2), 2026-11-22 at 20:32, after the trace sweep of boss replay 2 (its section 6, note of 20:30) and before any fight of this comparison is played. Sections 1–4 are not edited after the run starts; corrections go in section 6 with the reason.

## 1. Question and hypotheses
The fight search scores each candidate action on six sampled worlds and takes the best mean (`tools/selfplay.py scored_search`). In a fight whose rollouts are all lost, a world's value is −100 minus the enemies' HP left at death, and a mean of six such values moves by tens of points from one draw of worlds to the next, which is more than one card is worth. The trace sweep found what that costs: on the Agent's 22 traced Test Subject fights (the `mcw6paf` fights of boss replay 2 with every decision kept), 62 of 199 turns were ended with a playable card in hand that the energy left pays for (37 of them with energy left, the others with cards that cost nothing), in 20 of the 22 fights; the margin by which ending the turn was scored above the best card was 23 points at the median and under 30 points in 30 of 50; every candidate's rollouts were lost at 735 of 816 scored decisions. One turn was ended with 80 Block and a Body Slam+ that costs nothing in hand. All play candidates of such a turn are finished by the same rule planner, so the choice is in effect between two lines, ending the turn now or letting the planner go on, and it is made on a difference the six worlds do not resolve.

The correction (`e`): where the search's best candidate is ending the turn and the rule planner would play a card (its next action in the rollouts' mode, potions left out), the planner's card is played unless ending the turn is clearly better: the mean of the per-world differences between the two candidates (they share their sample seeds) has to exceed 2 of its standard errors. It applies only where at least one sampled world of either candidate was lost; where every world of both is won the values are HP left, which six worlds tell apart, and the search stands as before. Nothing else changes: the same candidates, the same rollouts, no extra rollout. After the planner's card the next decision is searched as before, so a turn still ends when the search and the planner agree on it, or when the search is clear.

- **H1 (HP removed):** on the Test Subject situations the search with the check removes a larger share of the three forms' HP than the search without it (a win counts as all of it).
- **H2 (wins):** it wins more of these fights.
- A condition and not a hypothesis: on ordinary fights of all room types the check costs nothing that can be seen (section 4).

Choices made before the run and not tuned on it: 2 standard errors (about a one-sided 5% test on six paired worlds); the planner's card and not the best-scored card (inside the noise the scores carry no order; the planner is the rollouts' own policy and reads the powers that punish card plays, for example the Test Subject's Enrage); the scope (any lost world of the two candidates) and not only decisions where every world is lost, because a count of won worlds out of six is the noisiest value of all.

## 4. Metrics and tests
- **H1:** share of the three forms' HP removed, per situation the mean over its sample seeds, over Sets T1 and T2 together (28 situations): one-sided sign test at 0.05 over the situations whose two arms differ. **H2:** fights won per situation, the same test. Reported with them: fights won per arm and set, the form a lost fight ended in, turns per fight.
- **Set B:** fights won per arm; over the fights both arms win, the mean difference in HP left with a bootstrap 95% interval; over all fights the mean difference in priced value; the same by room type.
- Per arm and set: how often the check was asked, how often it kept the end of the turn, played the planner's card, or found the planner's card absent from the candidates (`end_checks` in the fight records); search errors; seconds per decision.
- A situation or fight with a host error in either arm is left out of the paired tests and reported.
- **What follows.** The check becomes the default of the fight search for everything started afterwards if all of these hold: H1 holds; the arm with the check wins at least as many Test Subject fights as the arm without; on Set B it wins at least as many fights as the arm without, less one at most; and on Set B's fights that both arms win its mean HP left is not lower by more than 1.0. Then the next free run-benchmark slot measures in whole runs what it is worth in wins, as for the forms option. If H1 does not hold, the check stays off and the noise is taken up by another registered route (more worlds for the candidates that are close). If H1 holds and Set B's conditions do not, the check stays off in general and a version limited to boss rooms is registered on its own.

## 6. Deviations and notes
- 2026-11-22 20:32, before the start: the tests named in section 2. Unit cases on made-up values (`tools/tests/test_selfplay.py`, eight): inside the noise the planner's card is played; a clear end of turn is kept, and so is a small edge that holds in every world when one world is lost; nothing changes where every world of both candidates is won, where the best is a card, where the planner ends the turn or names a potion, or with the check off; a second copy of the planner's card counts as that card and another card or target does not; unfinished rollouts are left out and three worlds are needed; a card whose step failed is not played. One fight outside every registered set (The Kin, an act 1 boss situation of run benchmark 9, `data/sim/boss-fights-rb9.json` index 3, seed 7900003, the check on, `tools/fight_trace.py --end-turn-z 2`): played to its end without a host error (won, 34 decisions); the check was asked once and played the planner's card once (an Anger that costs nothing, scored 15 points behind ending the turn). One fight says nothing about the check's worth and is not read as a result.
- The check is off by default (`tools/selfplay.py END_TURN_Z`, `SIM_END_TURN_Z` in the environment), so run benchmark 17, which starts while this comparison runs, plays without it; a run benchmark's manifest records the setting from now on (`end_turn_z`).
