# Deck Archetypes: Regent (v0.107.1)

50 final decks with 5+ non-starter cards, clustered by card overlap (Jaccard distance, average linkage). k = 3, chosen by silhouette among 3–6. Silhouette scores: k=2: 0.05, k=3: 0.04, k=4: 0.03, k=5: 0.04, k=6: 0.04, k=7: 0.04, k=8: 0.03.

Low silhouette scores (under about 0.25) mean the clusters overlap a lot: the Player's decks blend archetypes more than they commit to one. Names below are working labels from the defining cards; they get checked against card text in Phase 2.

> **Statistical cautions.** (1) Selection bias: what gets offered depends on earlier picks and relics.
> (2) Survivorship: late-floor numbers come only from runs that got there. (3) Ascension confounds
> everything; compare within bands. (4) Small samples: show n next to every rate; win correlations are
> hypotheses. (5) Multiple comparisons: with ~100 cards, some will look significant by chance.
